Orders & delivery

My order hasn't arrived.

Open the order in the app to see live status and your rider on the map. You can chat with the shop or rider from the order. If it is late or missing, tap Report a problem.

When do I confirm delivery?

After the rider reports delivery and you have actually received everything. Confirming releases payment to the shop and rider.

A rider isn't being assigned.

We keep searching nearby and widen the radius. You will get an update if none is available and can choose pickup instead.

Payments & wallet

M-Pesa prompt didn't appear.

Check you entered the right Safaricom number, you have network, and try again. If money left your account but the order shows unpaid, contact us with the M-Pesa message.

How do I withdraw earnings?

Open Wallet, tap Withdraw and choose M-Pesa. Funds that are still in escrow become available after the order or session is confirmed.

Why is my payment 'on hold'?

Payments are held until the order or consultation is confirmed complete, or while a dispute is reviewed.

Consultations

How do I book an expert?

Open Find an expert, choose a professional and a type (chat, call or farm visit), describe the problem and send the request. You pay after the expert accepts.

The expert hasn't replied.

Requests expire if not accepted. You are not charged until you pay after acceptance. You can pick another expert.

Account & safety

I can't log in.

Use Forgot password with your verified phone number. If you changed numbers, email us.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps on Delete your account.

How do I report unsafe behaviour or fake products?

Use Report a problem on the order, or email us with details and photos. Serious safety issues are prioritised.
