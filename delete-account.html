Delete your Farm2Wealth account

App: Farm2Wealth · Last updated: 8 October 2026

You can ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time. This page explains how, what we remove, and what we are required to keep.

How to request deletion

  1. Before you start: finish or cancel any open orders, deliveries, consultations or disputes, and withdraw any money left in your wallet.
  2. Send the request using the form below, or email njuguna.ezekiel.mburu@gmail.com from the email on your account, or use Profile → Help & support → Email inside the app.
  3. We verify it is you (for example with a one-time code sent to the phone number on the account).
  4. We delete your data and confirm by email or SMS, within [30 days].

This opens your email app with the request filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, photo, phone number, email, county
  • Saved farm, shop and other locations, and location history
  • Verification documents (ID, licences, logbook, passport photo)
  • Chat messages, produce listings, product listings, articles and photos you uploaded
  • Push-notification tokens and device data

What we keep, and for how long

The law and our need to prevent fraud mean we must keep some records. These are kept securely, restricted, and not used for anything else:

  • Transaction, payment and payout records: [7 years] (tax and financial regulations)
  • Dispute and complaint records linked to a transaction: [7 years]
  • Information needed to block fraud or banned accounts: [as long as needed]
  • Ratings you gave others may stay in anonymised form so the other person's score stays accurate

Just want less data used?

You can also withdraw location and marketing consent in your phone settings or in the app, without deleting your account. See our Privacy Policy for all your rights.
