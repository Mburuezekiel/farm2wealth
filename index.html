One app, four roles

Everyone in the farm value chain gets a defined place, and a way to earn.

Farmers

  • Buy seeds, fertilizer and tools from verified agrovets near you
  • Track your delivery live on the map
  • Book paid consultations with agronomists and vets
  • List and sell your own produce
  • Weather, season alerts and a free knowledge base

Agrovets

  • A digital storefront and product catalogue
  • Order dashboard and low-stock alerts
  • Delivery without managing riders yourself
  • Sales reports and payouts to M-Pesa

Bike riders

  • See pickup, drop-off and your payout before you accept
  • Navigate, deliver and confirm with proof of delivery
  • Earnings wallet with M-Pesa withdrawals
  • Ratings that help you get more jobs

Professionals

  • A verified profile with your specialties and rates
  • Go online when you are free, accept requests
  • Chat, call or visit the farm, and get paid per session
  • Publish articles to build your reputation

Questions

Is Farm2Wealth free to use?

Downloading and creating an account is free. Fees are a transparent commission on completed orders and paid consultations, and any delivery fee is shown before you confirm.

How do I pay?

With M-Pesa. You approve an STK push on your phone. Your money is held safely by the platform and is only released to the shop, rider or professional once you confirm everything went well.

What if something goes wrong with my order?

Open the order and tap Report a problem. Choose what went wrong, add photos if you like, and your payment stays on hold while our team reviews it.

Who can sign up?

Anyone aged 18 or over in Kenya. Pick your role when you register: Farmer, Agrovet, Rider or Professional. Agrovets, Riders and Professionals submit documents (such as ID and licences) so they can be verified.

How do I become a rider, agrovet or professional?

Choose that role at sign-up and complete the short onboarding. We review your documents before your profile goes live with a verified badge.

Is my data safe?

We ask for consent first, collect only what we need, and use your location to match you with nearby shops, experts and riders, not to sell to anyone. Read the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete your account for the steps and what happens to your data.
