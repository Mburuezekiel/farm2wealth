4. Who we share data with

  • The people in your transaction. For an order, the shop, the buyer and the assigned rider see what they need to complete it (for example name, delivery area, order items). A rider's live location is visible only to the farmer and agrovet on that active delivery.
  • Service providers who process data for us under agreements: Safaricom M-Pesa (payments); cloud hosting and file storage; Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications); maps and geocoding; SMS gateway (OTP and alerts); analytics and crash reporting. [List your actual vendors]
  • Authorities where the law requires, or to protect rights, safety and prevent fraud.
  • Business transfers if Kilimo is merged or acquired, with the same protections.
  • Aggregated insights. We may publish or share statistics that are anonymised and cannot identify you.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Location

Location is optional and separately consented to. We use it to show shops, professionals and riders near you, to dispatch deliveries, and to show live tracking during an active order. Riders and available professionals can be asked to share location while online; you can go offline or turn off permission in your phone settings at any time. Some features (like nearby search or taking delivery jobs) will not work without it. We do not use your location for advertising.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you can: be informed about how your data is used; access your data; correct inaccurate data; delete your data; object to or restrict processing; receive a copy of your data in a portable format; and withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect past processing). To use these rights, see Delete your account or email support@inuafund.co.ke. We will respond within the time the law allows. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at odpc.go.ke.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and sensitive data such as ID documents is protected with encrypted storage and restricted access, limited to verification staff. We use access controls, audit logs and regular reviews. No system is perfectly secure, so please protect your login and tell us at once if you suspect misuse.

9. Children

Kilimo is for people aged 18 and over because it involves financial transactions. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Transfers outside Kenya

Some providers (for example cloud hosting or push notifications) may process data outside Kenya. Where that happens, we require appropriate safeguards as set out in the Data Protection Act.

11. This website

This website does not use tracking cookies or advertising trackers. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to fetch them. Emails you send us are used only to reply to you.

12. Changes

We will update this policy as the app grows. For significant changes we will notify you in the app and, where required, ask for your consent again. The date at the top shows the latest version.
